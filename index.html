sans_bass

Drop a song, or a .zip of stems

One audio file — a whole song — plays as a single track, and can be split into six stems right here in the browser. A .zip of stems already separated loads them as one lane each.

To make a zip: on macOS, right-click a folder of stems and choose Compress. Dropping the folder itself is not supported.

Everything is decoded locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
